Red Room respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide to us. 
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information when you interact with Red Room, 
including when you provide your phone number or email address and consent to receive communications from us.

1. Information We Collect

   Depending on how you interact with Red Room, we may collect:
   Name and contact information;
   Mobile phone number;
   Email address;
   Information you provide when contacting or communicating with us;
   Information related to your requests, purchases, appointments, or other interactions with Red Room; and
   Information you voluntarily provide through our website, forms, social media, or other communication channels.

2. How We Use Your Information

   We may use the information we collect to:
   Provide products, services, appointments, or information you request;
   Communicate with you regarding your inquiries or transactions;
   Send customer service and account-related communications;
   Send promotional or marketing communications when you have provided the appropriate consent;
   Send SMS/text messages when you have specifically opted in to receive them;
   Send email communications when you have provided your email address and, where required, consented to receive marketing emails;
   Improve our services and customer experience; and
   Comply with applicable laws and legal obligations.

3. SMS/Text Messaging Privacy

   If you provide your mobile phone number and expressly opt in to receive SMS messages from Red Room, you consent to 
   receive recurring text messages related to the purpose described at the time of opt-in.
   Message frequency may vary. You may receive up to 10 messages per month.
   Message and data rates may apply.
   Your mobile phone number and SMS opt-in information will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or 
   affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. We may share information with service providers that 
   assist us in operating our business and providing messaging services, but those providers may only use the information 
   to perform services on our behalf.
   Your consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services from Red Room.
   You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to a message from Red Room. After you send STOP, 
   you will receive confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and you will no longer receive SMS messages from us 
   unless you subsequently provide new consent.
   For help, reply HELP to a Red Room text message or contact us using the information below.

4. Email Communications

   If you provide your email address, we may use it to communicate with you regarding your transactions, requests, 
   services, or other interactions with Red Room.
   Where required by applicable law, we will obtain your consent before sending promotional or marketing emails.
   You may unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link included in the email or 
   by contacting us using the information below. You may continue to receive certain transactional or service-related 
   emails when necessary to provide services you have requested.

5. Sharing of Information

   Red Room does not sell your personal information.
   In particular, Red Room does not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or SMS consent information with third 
   parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
   We may disclose personal information to trusted service providers that help us operate our business, such as website 
   hosting providers, customer relationship management platforms, email service providers, SMS/messaging providers, 
   payment processors, scheduling providers, and other vendors that perform services on our behalf.
   We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, legal process, 
   governmental requests, protect our rights, prevent fraud or abuse, or protect the safety of our customers or others.

6. Data Security

   We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information against 
   unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmitting or storing information 
   electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

   We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain appropriate 
   business and transaction records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.
   SMS consent and opt-out information may be retained as necessary to maintain suppression records and demonstrate 
   compliance with applicable requirements.

8. Your Choices

   You may request to:
   Stop receiving SMS marketing messages by replying STOP;
   Request help with SMS communications by replying HELP;
   Unsubscribe from marketing emails using the unsubscribe mechanism provided in the email; or
   Contact us to request information about the personal information we maintain about you, subject to applicable law.

9. Children's Privacy

   Red Room does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. 
   If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without appropriate consent, 
   we will take reasonable steps to delete that information.
